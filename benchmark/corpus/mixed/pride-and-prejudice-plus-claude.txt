Elizabeth, for her part, found the whole affair more amusing than her mother could bear. She watched Mrs. Bennet pace between the window and the mantelpiece, rehearsing compliments for a gentleman she had never met, and reflected that a wealthy neighbour was apparently more exciting to the household than any book in her father's library. When Jane gently suggested that Mr. Bingley might not care for a crowded family dinner, their mother only waved the objection away, already certain of the outcome.
